Mixing dangers

  1. 1Read
  2. →
  3. 2Watch
  4. →
  5. 3Quiz

Never mix cleaning products unless the label says it is safe. Some combinations create toxic gases. Bleach should never be mixed with ammonia or with acidic cleaners such as vinegar or some bathroom products. Always read the label, use one product at a time, rinse surfaces between products, and ventilate the area. If you smell something strong or feel dizzy, leave the area and tell your supervisor.
